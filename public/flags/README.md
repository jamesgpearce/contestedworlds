# Flag identifiers

SVGs from [flag-icons v7.3.2](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/v7.3.2/flags/4x3), by Panayiotis Lipiridis and contributors. See the adjacent MIT LICENSE.

These contemporary flags identify the atlas's political-power and island rows; they are not flags reconstructed for every historical date. The UK identifier includes English administrations before 1707. Courland and Gran Colombia use lettermarks; Indigenous societies, shared rule and independence use non-national symbols.

The Order of Malta is identified by its eight-pointed white Maltese cross on red, drawn directly in SVG in the power-symbol component. This follows the flag of the Order's works described in its [official flags and emblems reference](https://www.orderofmalta.int/government/flags-emblems/); it is an identifying emblem, not a reconstruction of its historical state flag or the modern Maltese national flag.

Island rows use contemporary national or territorial flags. Trinidad and Tobago, Antigua and Barbuda, Saint Kitts and Nevis, and the US Virgin Islands share flags within each country or territory. Guadeloupe, Saint-Martin and Saint-Barthélemy use France's flag; Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba use the Dutch flag. These identifiers do not imply historical sovereignty.
