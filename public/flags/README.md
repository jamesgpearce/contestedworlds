# Flag identifiers

Nine SVGs from [flag-icons v7.3.2](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/v7.3.2/flags/4x3), by Panayiotis Lipiridis and contributors. See the adjacent MIT LICENSE.

These contemporary flags identify the atlas's broad political-power rows; they are not flags reconstructed for every historical date. The UK identifier includes English administrations before 1707. Courland and Gran Colombia use lettermarks; Indigenous societies, shared rule and independence use non-national symbols.

The simple Order of Malta state flag is drawn as a red field and white cross in the power-symbol component, following the Order's [official flags and emblems description](https://www.orderofmalta.int/government/flags-emblems/). It is distinct from the modern Maltese national flag.
