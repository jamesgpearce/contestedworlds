# Third-party credits

- **flag-icons** — SVG national and territorial flags, MIT. [Project](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons); the full notice is retained in [public/flags/LICENSE](public/flags/LICENSE). The generated share card embeds a subset of these flags.
- **Natural Earth** — public-domain 1:110m coastline data, clipped for the locator map. The source link and attribution are recorded in `src/lib/coastlines.json` and displayed beside the map. [Terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
- **Lucide** — interface icons supplied by `lucide-react`, ISC. [License](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **Preact** — browser renderer and React compatibility layer, MIT. [License](https://github.com/preactjs/preact/blob/main/LICENSE).

The React/Vite application and other npm dependencies retain their own licenses in their packages. `package-lock.json` records their exact versions. Historical publications are cited in `data/sources.json`; inclusion in the bibliography does not relicense their text.
